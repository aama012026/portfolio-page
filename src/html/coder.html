Riftbound Collection Tracker

This WIP project tracks your collection of Riftbound TCG cards. It includes a lexer and parser that interprets the rules text on the cards in order to hide reminder text to be revealed on hover, mark up keywords, etc.

The project is written in typescript, sits on a hetzner server in germany running debian, and is built in bun with data-star as its framework. The data is stored in and queried from a mariaDB relational database colocated on the server and communicating over localhost.

You'll have to forgive the current, somewhat garish, color combination on the top sort bar and bottom filter bar. The boxes in the sort bar can be dragged to reorder the sort order. This is all done through data-star signals, queried on the backend and patched on the client with a morphing strategy over SSE. The filters query and card count tracking has yet to be implemented.

If you're interested in looking at data-star in action, take a look at the templates.html file and look for data-attributes like data-on:<event>, data-signals:<signal-name>, data-bind="<expression>", etc. The file includes custom variable markup used by my tiny HTML builder to generate js HTML-string construction functions.

If you want to look at the backend side, take a look at the SSE construction in sse.ts. The events are streamed to the client with a simple ReadableStream object. To se my go at writing SQL stored procedures and functions, have a look at either createProcedures.ts or createFunctions.ts under src/schema/.
